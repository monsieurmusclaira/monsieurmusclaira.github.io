Moonlight Woman is a fiction short directed by Griet Elsen, in which Sarah’s carefully ordered life with Willem begins to come undone when a mysterious sound calls her into the night. Drawn through the forest to a moonlit lake, she encounters a sensual, wordless desire rising from the depths. The cinematography follows her transformation through the contrast between domestic warmth and the wild, golden darkness beyond it.
